## How to publish (Blogger post-by-email)
1. Open Gmail
2. To: your Blogger email address (e.g. kottalgi.2022.auto@blogger.com)
3. Subject: exact title from the post
4. Body: paste the content AFTER the frontmatter (from FreeDailyPro.com -- onward). Or paste full markdown and clean in Blogger.
5. Attach the matching .webp image
6. Labels: copy from labels: line in frontmatter (comma separated)
7. Send
8. Open Blogger → check draft/post → set publish date if scheduling → Publish
