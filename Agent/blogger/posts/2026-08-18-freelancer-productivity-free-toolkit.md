1. Collect client chapters or assets.  
2. Merge into one PDF.  
3. Compress so it emails cleanly.  
4. Prepare cover or portfolio images with private image tools.  
5. Count words and polish text.  
6. Deliver and invoice.  
7. Star the whole set in Favorites so tomorrow’s work starts on your personal tools page.
