## Style quick differences students mix up

APA often prioritizes date and author-date in-text citations. MLA often prioritizes author-page for literary and humanities work. Chicago notes-bibliography can use footnotes; Chicago author-date looks closer to APA. Harvard variants differ by institution. The [Citation Generator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/citation-generator) helps you pick a style deliberately—then you still match your syllabus.

If your professor posts a local style sheet that conflicts with a general generator output, the local sheet wins. Generators are accelerators, not final authority for course-specific quirks.
