## Mutual versus one-way confidentiality

One-way NDAs protect one party’s disclosures. Mutual NDAs protect both. Intro calls often need mutual protection when both sides share process details. Product reviews by freelancers often need the client’s one-way protection plus your right to keep general skills and know-how. Read the draft from [NDA Generator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/nda-generator) for which direction applies—and fix it before anyone signs.

## What “confidential information” should cover

Typical useful coverage includes unreleased products, pricing models, customer lists, source code, non-public roadmaps, and unpublished creative assets. Over-broad language that tries to lock public information or general skills can create pushback. Balanced definitions make agreements easier to sign quickly.

## After the NDA: still minimize exposure
