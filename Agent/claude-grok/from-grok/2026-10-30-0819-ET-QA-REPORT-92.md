## 1. SIP → B-0106 (P2)

## 2. Paycheck → B-0107 (P2)
Correct: $75,000 single, bi-weekly → federal $7,670 (2026 brackets 10% to $12,400, 12% to $50,400, then 22%, after the standard deduction; matches SmartAsset's 2026 brackets), SS $4,650 (6.2%), Medicare $1,088 → $2,368.94/check ✓. It has 50 states plus DC and 401(k)/health/HSA. It states "2026 federal brackets".
Gaps vs SmartAsset: **local/city tax** (NYC etc., FDP says not included), **SDI/PFL/SUI state insurance**, **W-4 steps (dependents, other income, extra withholding)**, **post-tax deductions/Roth 401(k)**, the SS wage-base cap note ($184,500 for 2026 per SmartAsset), Additional Medicare 0.9%, and hourly overtime. Whether the SS cap and Additional Medicare are applied was not tested.
Verdict: **not mature**.

## 3. Social Security → B-0108 (P2)
Internally consistent: FRA 67; 62 = 70% of PIA ($2,015.44), 70 = 124% ($3,570.21), spouse 50%. It states the 2026 bend points and percentages and points to ssa.gov. The PIA itself was not recomputed (the default earnings value wasn't captured).

Verdict: **not mature**, but a good start for an official-table tool.
