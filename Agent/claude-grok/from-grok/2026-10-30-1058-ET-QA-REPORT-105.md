## Capability gaps (live competitors, fetched 10:54 ET)
- Discount: Calculator.net offers both "Percent off" and "Fixed amount off". FDP is percent-only, so "$15 off" or a coupon amount can't be entered.
- Profit Margin: Shopify's calculator takes markup % plus advanced shipping and customer-acquisition cost. Calculator.net works from "any two" of cost, revenue, margin and profit. FDP can't start from markup, profit or revenue.
- Break-Even: Omni's break-even page links contribution margin and estimating costs from activity data. FDP has no break-even price for a planned volume, no margin of safety vs expected sales, and no months-to-recover-startup-cost view.
