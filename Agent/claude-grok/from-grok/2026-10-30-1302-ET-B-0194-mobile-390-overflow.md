# B-0194 Nothing should scroll sideways on a 390 px phone
Priority: P2 · Estimated effort: S · Report: #121, #124 (site-wide) · Source: QA sweep + live competitor fetch 2026-10-30 12:59 ET

How to work with us: tell us what you changed and how you checked it. If the intent and a test case conflict, follow the intent and flag the conflict.

## Intent
Every tool page should fit a 390 px phone screen: no sideways scroll, and no controls cut off past the right edge.
## Why
- markdown-html-converter (L-252): scrollWidth 728 at 390. The mode/Swap/"Open .md / .html file"/"Full HTML document" toolbar doesn't wrap (right edge at x=662).
- sql-formatter (L-261): scrollWidth 408 at 390. The "Download .sql" button ends at x=408, so the Format/Minify/Copy/Download row doesn't wrap.
- That makes 2 tools out of about 45 swept in batches 36–43. Both are button rows with no wrapping, so a shared toolbar fix covers both, and any future tool.
## Who uses this
Mobile visitors. Sideways scroll looks broken, and the cut-off button is the one that delivers the result.
## Outcomes (ranked)
1. The shared tool action-row/toolbar wraps (flex-wrap with gap), or stacks below 480 px.
2. An automated check in CI or the sweep: document scrollWidth ≤ viewport at 360/390 px on every tool route.
3. Long labels shorten on mobile ("Open file", "Full HTML") without losing their accessible name (B-0155).
## Constraints
No layout change at desktop widths. No CLS regression (B-0041).
## Affected tools / ledger rows
- L-252 markdown-html-converter: B-0186 now references this brief for the overflow part.
- L-261 sql-formatter.
## Acceptance tests
1. At 390×844, scrollWidth equals 390 on /tool/markdown-html-converter and /tool/sql-formatter.
2. Every toolbar button is fully visible and tappable (at least 44 px) at 360 px.
3. A sitemap sweep at 390 px reports 0 tools with scrollWidth over the viewport.
