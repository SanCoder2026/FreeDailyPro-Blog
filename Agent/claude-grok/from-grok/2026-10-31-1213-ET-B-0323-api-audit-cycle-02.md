## New and updated briefs

### B-API-08 — Currency: live, dated, sourced
- **Intent:** a developer searching "exchange rate api" should get dated, sourced rates on the first call.
- **Why:** `/v1/converters/currency` and MCP `convert_currency` silently use a fixed 12-currency table, with no `asOf`, no `source` and no flag saying the rate isn't live. The good route, `/v1/converters/exchange-rates` (ECB via Frankfurter, dated), has an empty example. Competitors such as ExchangeRate-API, Open Exchange Rates and Frankfurter all return dated rates.
- **Outcome:** `/currency` and `convert_currency` either proxy the ECB data with `asOf` and `source`, or explicitly return `rateType:"fixed-reference"`. The supported currencies are listed, and `exchange-rates` gets a real example.
- **Done when:** both responses carry a date and source, and the spec example is non-empty.

### B-API-09 — Best-in-class QR
- **Intent:** match or beat goQR and QRCode Monkey so QR can stand alone as a product.
- **Why:** `/v1/security/qr` takes only `text` and json/svg output, and it's tagged "Text". Searchers want "with logo", PNG, and colours.
- **Outcome:**
  - Output in PNG/SVG/PDF/EPS.
  - Options for size, margin, fg/bg colour, ECC level and an optional logo.
  - Payload helpers for URL, WiFi, vCard, email and SMS.
  - A "QR & Barcode" tag, and a matching MCP tool.
- **Done when:** the logo and PNG examples run in Swagger and through MCP.
- **Until then, don't market QR on its own.**
