## How to work with us

You know this codebase. Treat this as evidence and judgment, not orders. The user is a bookkeeper, a small-business owner, or someone filling in a loan or tax form. The promise in the title is **"Checked"**. Users will trust a green "Check passed" and stop looking, so the check matters more than any single parse rule.

What already works well:
- On the most common US layout (signed amount + running balance), every one of 70 rows across 5 pages was exact, and wrapped descriptions joined correctly (A, L).
- Privacy holds: no request body carried statement text and nothing was stored in local or session storage (D-series privacy, P-series `posts:0`).
- The CSV has a UTF-8 BOM and guards formulas (`=`, `+`, `@`, `-` get a leading `'`).
- The Excel file has real dates (yyyy-mm-dd) and real numbers, with descriptions as strings.
- Scans are refused with a clear message.
- The German "1.200,00" is read as 1200 from a PDF and from pasted text.

The tool goes wrong in two ways:
1. **It sometimes says "Check passed" on a wrong table.** HSBC-style dates printed once per day, Barclays "Start balance", and French space thousands. Two causes: lines without a date are dropped without being counted, and the balance lines that would expose the error are themselves misread.
2. **Common layouts give wrong signs, dates and rows**, which the check then flags. This hurts the first impression.

## Findings (verified live as a guest)

**P1 #1: Per-page-load charging (owner rule).** D01: CSV of L 0/10 → 1/10 (1 POST). D02: Excel of the same table, still 1/10. D03: dropped F, downloaded CSV, dropped A, downloaded Excel, still 1/10 with no new POST. So 1 use bought **4 files from 3 statements**. After a reload (D04) a download cost again. `logUsageNow` (ToolPage) is used rather than a per-export key.

**P1 #2: "Check passed" on wrong tables.** The check is the product's promise, and it goes green while money is wrong:
- **HSBC-style (D):** banks that print the date once per day lose every following same-day row: 11 of 22 disappear, with no "skipped" note, because the skip counter only counts lines that start with a date. "01 Mar 26": the 2-digit year isn't consumed, so "26" starts every description. As a result "26 BALANCE BROUGHT FORWARD"/"CARRIED FORWARD" are not recognised and become transactions of −4,250.00 and −5,047.58. Shown: green "Check passed. All 3 running balances follow from the rows." Table total −7,905.32 vs true +797.58.
- **Barclays-style (E):** "Start balance" and "End balance" aren't in the opening/closing patterns (only "starting"/"ending"), so they become rows of −1,834.12 and −7,451.37 (sign guessed). They carry no balance, so the row check skips them, and no closing balance is known, so no total check runs. Green "All 17 running balances…"; total off by 9,285.49.
- **French space thousands (T05):** "Ancien solde 2 345,67 / Loyer -1 234,56 / Salaire +2 310,00 / Nouveau solde 3 421,11" parses to 345.67, −234.56, 310.00, 421.11. These balance, so the check is green with every number wrong; "Loyer -1" and "Salaire +2" go into the descriptions. In the PDF (H), 7 of 14 amounts are split this way.

Fix direction:
- count non-date lines that carry amounts as "not used" and fail the check when any exist, or carry the last date forward;
- consume a 2-digit year after "dd Mon";
- add "start/end balance", "balance brought/carried forward" after a date, and "Kontostand am" / "solde créditeur au";
- accept a space or NBSP (U+00A0/U+202F) as a thousands separator in EU mode;
- never show green when any row has a guessed sign or a phantom balance-like description.

**P2 #3: Chase-style period line becomes a phantom row.** "March 1, 2026 through March 31, 2026 · Account Number…" starts with a date, so it collects the summary lines until "Deposits and Additions 12,721.18" and becomes a −12,721.18 row (sign guessed) on row 1. The check says "12721.18 away" without pointing at it. Chase-style statements print the period this way at the top of the page.

**P2 #4: Signs on unsigned columns and explicit "+".**
- An explicit "+" is dropped before the sign logic ("+150.00" → −150.00, T04).
- Separate Deposits/Withdrawals columns lose their position in the joined line, so signs fall back to keywords: Wells "Zelle From", "Purchase Return"; BoA "INTEREST PAYMENT"; French "REMISE CHEQUE" (a cheque deposit).
- Suggest: treat "+" as money in; use the x-position of the amount relative to the column headers (the text items have x); add the common abbreviations.

**P2 #5: Date order.**
- dd.mm.yyyy and dd/mm/yyyy are read month-first unless some day is above 12, even when the number style was detected as European (F: 18/20 dates wrong with a green check; G, H, T05 the same). A statement for the 1st–12th, or a short export, is enough to trigger this.
- "15 Dec 25" ignores the year (T06).
- yyyy/mm/dd is not read (T07, I).
- Across the year end, the Year box applies one year to all rows (K: Dec → 2026 by default; Year 2025 → January becomes 2025-01-03).
- Suggest: prefer day-first when the number style is European; use the statement period line for the year; roll the year back when months go backwards.

**P2 #6: European rows dropped without a note.**
- Amounts followed by "EUR"/"€" (T03: 0 rows; G: 2 of 10 lost).
- Amounts without cents ("1.200", T08).
- Sparkasse "Kontostand am …" not recognised, so no check at all on G.
- "dd.mm." dates leave ". 01.03." in the description.
- These rows don't start the "skipped lines" note.

**P2 #7: Correcting a cell the European way, and the CSV in European spreadsheets.**
- The Amount cell's onBlur does `Number(value.replace(/,/g,''))`: "-1.200,00" → −1.20; "-1200,00" → −120000.00 (V03). This is the #84 pattern in the edit path. Parse the edit with the detected number style.
- The CSV always uses "," as separator and "." as decimal; in LibreOffice de-DE all amounts import as text (SUM 0).
- Suggest a "CSV for Excel (Europe)" option with ";" and decimal comma, or point EU users to the Excel download, which is correct.

**P2 #8: Card statements: the check can't pass, and the advice is backwards.**
- With Previous/New Balance printed (normal), the card-layout logic is off (it needs no opening balance).
- Unflipped: purchases −, payment −500.00 and return −42.00 (should be +), check 1,168.22 below.
- Flipped: everything +, check 1,084.00 above (V01).
- The page ("tick the box that flips the signs") and the guide ("Leaving card sign flip off so purchases look like deposits") tell users to flip. Flipping makes purchases positive, i.e. money in under the tool's own rule "Money in is positive".
- Suggest a card mode where the check is Previous + charges − credits = New Balance, and the export uses one convention.

**P2 #9: Phones.** Once the table appears, the page is wider than the screen (iPhone SE 453/320 px, iPhone 13 453/390 px, Pixel 7 layout widened to 454). The cause is the 30 rem table and an absolutely positioned "Remove" sr-only label escaping the scroll box. Descriptions show about 8 characters, and amounts are hidden past the edge. Suggest a stacked card per row on phones and `position:relative` on the scroll container.

**P2 #10: Locale pages.** All 10 hreflang pages keep the English title, H1, intro, drop zone, labels and FAQ. Only site chrome and the trial strip are translated. /de and /fr use the same Detect defaults as English, with no day-first or 1.234,56 preference (code: state starts at "auto").

**P3 #11: Text joining.**
- Every text item is joined with a space, so CJK splits ("東京 ラ ー メン"), Arabic comes out as reversed presentation forms, and ligatures split ("Spring fi eld").
- Section/page headers that don't match the skip list are glued into the previous description ("INTEREST PAYMENT Withdrawals and other subtractions", "… Check Number").
- Digits-only continuation lines (reference numbers) are dropped.
- Suggest: join by x-gap, NFKC-normalise, keep RTL order, and widen the header skip list.

**P3 #12: SEO.** OG image is the site default. Otherwise good.

**P3 #13: Copy and links.**
- "the check updates as you type" (guide): it updates on blur.
- The cap dialog promises removal of the mark "from your PDFs and images", which is irrelevant for CSV.
- The FAQ, the How-to and the scan toast say "OCR first" with no link to OCR PDF / Image to Text.
- "More from PDF Tools" shows Merge/Split/Compress instead of PDF to Excel / OCR / CSV Converter.
- File names are always statement.csv / statement.xlsx; suggest the period, e.g. statement-2026-03.csv.

**P3 #14: Counter display.** After the reload (D04), the strip jumped 1/10 → 10/10 on one download (server sync; as on #84). Display only.

**P3 #15:** axe desktop `label-content-name-mismatch` (serious, `.right-2`) and `region`. There is no "add row" (V05), so a missing transaction can only be added by editing the paste box, which re-parses and drops cell edits (code: the table resets from the parse result).
