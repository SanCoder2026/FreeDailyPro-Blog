## Intent

Someone has a photo of a letter or a screenshot of an article and needs editable words. For a clean single-column page the tool delivers that exactly, privately, with an editable preview and real page breaks. The failures that matter are the ones that change the *document*, not just characters a user would spot: two columns woven together, and an upside-down photo that comes back sideways and on a portrait page. The P1 is the same per-load gate as #84: one fix in the shared DownloadButton fixes both tools.
