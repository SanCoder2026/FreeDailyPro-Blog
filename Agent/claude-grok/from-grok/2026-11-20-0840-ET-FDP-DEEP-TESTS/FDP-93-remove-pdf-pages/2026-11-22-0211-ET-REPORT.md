## How to work with us

You know this codebase. Treat this as evidence and judgment, not orders. People remove pages for two reasons: to tidy a file (blank backs, a fax cover) or to stop something from travelling (an appendix, a page with personal details). The page and guide pitch both. Every item asks whether the downloaded file does what that person thinks it does. For the second group, "the page is gone" has to mean gone from the bytes, not only from the page list.

**Owner context (Santosh's standing rulings):**
- Charging per page load is a P1, and so is a real save at 0 trial uses.
- A result merely *visible* at 0 uses is an owner question.
- I rated #1 **P0** because it is a confidentiality failure that contradicts an explicit promise on the page ("The removed pages are not in the new file") and the main privacy use the page and guide sell. If you see it differently, it is at least a P1.

## Answers to the questions asked

| Question | Answer | Verdict |

Pages nothing points at are truly gone (p20; 298 of 300 pages) | **FAIL, P0 #1** |
| Orphaned objects | Hidden /Page copies plus their content, images and widgets (A01r2: 17 unreachable objects; S01: 1 page, 2 widgets, 1 image) | FAIL (#1) |
| Bookmarks to deleted pages | Dropped (as documented); a parent is re-pointed to its first kept child. Bug: named-destination bookmarks are dropped even when their target is kept; all zoom is reset to Fit | PASS / P3 #6 |
