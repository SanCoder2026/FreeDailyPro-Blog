## Check 20: does Organize add enough over the Professional tools to be Premium?

**Verdict: not today.**

What it adds over remove / rotate / split / extract, all verified live:
- one visual pass that combines reorder, delete, rotate, duplicate and blank;
- drag, Reverse, multi-select, and Undo/Redo across all steps;
- form-field renaming on duplicates.

Why that isn't enough for 3× the price ($3 / $9.99 against Professional's $1 / $4.99) with 2 trial uses instead of 10:
1. **Merge PDF, a Professional Tool, already has "Arrange all pages".** Verified live today: move earlier/later, rotate left **and** right, and delete per page; its "Choose pages" accepts repeats (`1,1,2`, so duplicates) and "blank page after odd" (#3 report). Merge needs 2 files, but adding any one-page PDF and deleting its page in Arrange gives a single-file organizer: book.pdf + one.pdf, delete one.pdf p1 and book p5, move p20 first → 19 pages in the right order, "Merge 2 PDFs" enabled. The download was not verified (pool at 10/10, see RERUN). So Professional already covers almost everything except "insert blank anywhere", Undo/Redo and the thumbnail grid.
2. **Remove PDF Pages (Professional)** does delete-by-thumbnail. **Rotate PDF (Professional)** does more than Organize's rotation (any angle, odd/even, batch).
3. Competitors (iLovePDF Organize, PDF24 Rearrange) offer organize, *including adding another file* (iLovePDF), at no charge. A Premium label sets a higher bar: the file has to be flawless, and today it isn't (P0 #1, P1 #2).
4. It can't add pages from a second file. That is the most common thing people expect from an organizer, and what Merge's Arrange already does.

Options for the owner (any one makes the tiers coherent):
- (a) Move Organize to **Professional Tools** (same 10 uses, $1 / $4.99) and keep remove-pdf-pages as the quick tool that upsells it (DUPLICATES.md already suggests this).
- (b) Keep it Premium but earn it. Fix P0/P1 first, then add what Professional can't do: insert pages from other PDFs (merge inside Organize), insert from image/scan, keep links and bookmarks exactly (an "everything still works" promise), page-level labels, and split-on-save. Then Merge PDF's single-file Arrange becomes the light version.
- (c) Merge Organize and Merge PDF's Arrange into one editor, with the tier decided by features.

## Suggestions beyond the findings

- The fix for #1, #2 and #4 is one change: copy every source page in **one** `copyPages(src, allIndices)` call, so pdf-lib maps each /Dest and widget /P to the copied page; re-point duplicates and links to the first copy; then delete any /Page not in the tree before `save()`. Same family as #93 `Pt()`.
- After that, a line under Save such as "Links, bookmarks and form fields still work; removed pages are fully gone" would be a real trust point for a Premium tool, and a "wow" for the legal/HR users the redact blog targets.
