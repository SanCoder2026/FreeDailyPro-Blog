## How to work with us

You know this codebase. Treat this as evidence and judgment, not orders. The buyer is a paralegal or small-firm lawyer preparing a production. They need numbers that match the log, files that contain only what is produced, and nothing on the pages except the Bates label and legend counsel approved.

The desk's design fits that well:
- running ranges that skip withheld documents;
- refusing to build until page counts match;
- warnings for missing reasons and log fields;
- production log, withheld list, privilege log (CSV/Excel) and a generated cover letter;
- in-place stamping that respects rotation (`pageView`).

Three code paths could hurt a production badly and need a signed-in check first:
1. Hidden pages that came in from upstream tools (#93) ride through unstamped. **Replayed locally.**
2. Attachments and anything else that Redact PDF leaves pass straight into the production. **Replayed locally.**
3. Desk downloads don't use the `free` flag that #98 uses, so the trial badge path may stamp "Created with FreeDailyPro.com" onto produced documents.

**Owner context:**
- Workflow Products are tested up to sign-in/Stripe without Santosh's go-ahead.
- The FDP badge is intentional for trial outputs; here the question is whether it can reach a paying desk subscriber's production.
