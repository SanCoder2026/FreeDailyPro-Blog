## The temptation

You need an NDA tonight. A free template appears. You download it, forget who published it, upload it to a random PDF site to merge with your SOW, and send. That chain has three risks: wrong jurisdiction language, outdated clauses, and unnecessary file uploads.

## A safer free-document workflow

1. Choose templates from sources you trust (or your lawyer).  
2. Edit carefully.  
3. Convert/merge/compress with free private tools — https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf · https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/compress-pdf · https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/word-to-pdf  
4. Keep masters local.  
5. Do not upload signed agreements to unknown free converters.

## When free templates are often “enough”

Low-dollar, low-risk, standard scope work in a jurisdiction you understand, using a template reviewed once by a professional, with clear payment and IP basics. Free PDF packaging still matters.

## When free templates are not enough

High dollar amounts, regulated industries, employees, equity, international parties, complex IP, or anything that makes your stomach tight. That tightness is data. Hire help.
