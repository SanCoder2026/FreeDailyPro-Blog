How the Free Book Maker Works (It’s Ridiculously Simple)

1. Open the Book Maker tool. 2. Enter your book title and author name. 3. Upload your chapters — PDF, Word (.docx), plain text, or even images. 4. Give each file a real chapter title (this becomes the entry in the Table of Contents). 5. Drag and drop to put the chapters in the perfect order. 6. Optionally add a front cover, spine and copyright page. 7. Click generate. Instantly receive a complete book with auto-generated Table of Contents and List of Images.

You can choose real book trim sizes or classic US Letter / A4 for document-style content. The tool respects every page size you throw at it and produces a polished, ready-to-review draft.
