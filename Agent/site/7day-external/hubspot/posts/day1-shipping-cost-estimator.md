Marketing loves “free shipping.” Finance loves knowing what that costs. FreeDailyPro’s [Shipping Cost Estimator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/shipping-cost-estimator) helps ops estimate package cost from size and weight before the promise goes live.

## Ecommerce workflow
1. Measure a typical parcel.  
2. Enter dimensions and weight in [Shipping Cost Estimator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/shipping-cost-estimator).  
3. Convert units with [Unit Converter](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/unit-converter) if needed.  
4. Model discount impact with [Percentage Calculator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/percentage-calculator).  
5. Decide free-shipping thresholds with eyes open.

[Calculators](https://www.freedailypro.com/category/calculators) · [multitool apps](https://www.freedailypro.com/multitool-apps).

## Limits
Carrier contracts, dimensional weight rules, and surcharges vary. This is estimation—not a binding rate quote from your negotiated account.
