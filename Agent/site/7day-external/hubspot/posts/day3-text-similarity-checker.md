Two landing pages that say almost the same thing confuse buyers and search engines. FreeDailyPro’s [Text Similarity Checker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/text-similarity-checker) is a pairwise overlap check for drafts—not a full plagiarism courtroom.

## Marketing ops workflow
1. Paste Draft A and Draft B.  
2. Review similarity in [Text Similarity Checker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/text-similarity-checker).  
3. Rewrite overlapping claims.  
4. Confirm lengths with [Word Counter](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/word-counter); use [Diff Checker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/diff-checker) for line-level edits.

## Limits
Similarity is not legal originality proof. Short product names will always overlap. Human judgment still owns brand voice.
