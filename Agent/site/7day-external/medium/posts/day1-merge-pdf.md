Clients rarely want five attachments. They want one packet: proposal, budget, timeline, appendix. FreeDailyPro’s [Merge PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf) tool lets you combine files in the browser, reorder them, and download a single PDF.

## The delivery problem freelancers actually have
You finish the work in different apps. Design exports one PDF. Sheets export another. Your scope notes live in a third. Emailing all three looks disorganized and invites “I missed the budget file” replies.

Merging is not glamorous. It is professional hygiene. One file, intentional order, one filename that matches the project.

## How to merge without chaos
1. Export each section as its own PDF.  
2. Open [Merge PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf) and add files in the order the client should read them.  
3. Reorder if the list arrived wrong—cover story first, appendix last.  
4. Merge and open the result. Spot-check page breaks and total page count.  
5. If the file is huge, run [Compress PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/compress-pdf) before send. If a section needs text edits, use [PDF to Word](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/pdf-to-word) on that section only, then re-export and re-merge.

Explore more under [PDF tools](https://www.freedailypro.com/category/pdf-tools) or [multitool apps](https://www.freedailypro.com/multitool-apps).

## Naming the packet
`Acme_Q4_Proposal_Packet.pdf` beats `final_merge_3.pdf`. Your sent folder should answer questions a month later.

## Limits
Merge does not redesign layouts, does not OCR scans into perfect text, and does not fix corrupt files. Password-protected inputs may need unlocking first. Extremely large books may be better handled offline.

## When not to merge
If the client’s portal requires separate uploads per field, keep files split. If legal needs separately signed instruments, do not combine them into one unsignable blob.
