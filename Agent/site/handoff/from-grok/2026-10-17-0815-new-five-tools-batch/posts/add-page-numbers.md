## Why bare PDFs fail in real reviews

Clients say "page 4" while your viewer shows a cover as page 1 and a blank scan as page 2. Without stamps, nobody shares a coordinate system. Legal teams lose minutes on every call. Board packs without numbers get printed, reordered, and re-argued. Numbering is not decoration. It is a shared index for humans who will never open the same viewer at the same zoom.

## Decide position and start number before you click

Write three choices on a sticky note before the tool opens. Position: bottom-center, bottom-right, or top-right to match house style. Start number: usually 1 on the first visible content page. Some packs keep a cover unnumbered and start body at 1—only if your portal allows a two-file or cover-split approach. Format: plain 1, 2, 3 unless a form demands "Page 1 of N".

If pages are sideways, fix them with [Rotate PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/rotate-pdf) first. Numbers on rotated pages look wrong in half of viewers and print upside down on some office devices.

## Build the stack, then number once

If the packet is still in pieces, assemble with [Merge PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf) in reading order before numbering. Numbering a partial stack forces a second pass and creates conflicting versions in email threads.

Size still high after merge? Compress after numbers only if stamps remain sharp—use [Compress PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/compress-pdf) carefully and zoom the footer at 150 percent. ## Numbered workflow

1. Open the final packet or merge parts first.
2. Confirm page count matches the sum of parts.
3. Open Add Page Numbers.
4. Choose position, start value, and format.
5. Apply and download.
6. Flip first, middle, and last pages full screen.
7. Search for a known clause and confirm the stamp matches the viewer page index.
8. Archive packet-numbered-DATE.pdf beside the unnumbered master.

## Worked example: 18-page vendor MSA

Cover (1) + terms (12) + exhibits (5) equals 18 pages. You start numbers at 1 on the cover for portal simplicity. Bottom-center placement. After apply, page 7 shows the liability cap exactly where your email said "see page 7." The reviewer stops asking which PDF you meant. A second reviewer on a phone sees the same stamp.

## Edge cases that break pagination

Blank scanner pages from phone capture inflate count—remove them before numbering. Landscape slides mixed with letter pages still number, but preview carefully. Existing faint numbers from an old export conflict with new stamps—prefer a clean export over stacking stamps. Some e-sign platforms re-flow pages; always download the platform final PDF if the deal is binding.

## Common mistakes

Numbering before the last exhibit arrives, then renumbering silently. Putting numbers in a margin that printers crop. Starting at 0 or double-counting a cover. Compressing so hard the footer becomes gray dust. Emailing both numbered and unnumbered files without labeling the authority copy. Trusting the OS thumbnail instead of opening page 1 and page N.

## FAQ

**Skip the cover?** Prefer splitting the cover out, or accept cover as page 1 if the portal is one-file only.

**Roman numerals for front matter?** Only if every reviewer agrees. Most small-business packets use arabic from page 1.

**After redaction?** Number after redaction and merge so stamps land on the external packet, not the internal draft.

**Headers already busy?** Use bottom-center to avoid colliding with letterhead.

## Section breaks and exhibits

If Exhibit A should restart at page 1, you need separate files or a tool path that supports section restarts. For many freelancers, one continuous sequence across the whole packet is clearer than clever restarts that confuse clients. Label exhibits in the filename and in a one-line cover email: "Pages 14 through 18 are Exhibit A."

## Print and accessibility

Printed packets hide numbers if the margin is under half an inch. Keep a safe margin. Visible numbers help people who print double-sided and people who magnify the page on a tablet. Test one printed page before a board meeting.

## Version control habits

When v2 replaces pages 9 through 11, renumber the whole file or keep continuous numbers and mark v2 in the filename. Do not leave two packets with identical names and different numbering schemes in the same folder. Put the date in ISO form so lexical sort matches time sort.

## Portal and email hygiene

Some portals strip annotations or re-encode PDFs. After upload, download the stored copy when the system allows and confirm numbers still appear. In email, cite "page 6 of Client-MSA-numbered-2026-11-05.pdf" so nobody opens last week's unnumbered draft.

## Mini case: board pack scramble

A board pack arrived as four emails. Staff merged in wrong order, numbered, then discovered the financials sat before the agenda. They had to re-merge and renumber under time pressure. Lesson: freeze order with numeric prefixes on disk (01-agenda, 02-minutes, 03-financials) before Add Page Numbers. Five minutes of naming beats twenty minutes of apology.

## Quality check list (two minutes)

Open page 1: number present, not clipped. Open a middle page: number advances by one. Open last page: matches total count. Zoom footer. Spot-check one internal cross-reference ("see page 12") against the stamp. Only then ship.
