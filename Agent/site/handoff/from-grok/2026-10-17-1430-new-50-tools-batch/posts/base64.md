## Binary safety habits for base64

Treat base64 as encoding, not encryption. Anyone can decode it. Do not place secrets in base64 and call them protected. When you paste encoded strings into tickets, confirm they are icons or non-sensitive fixtures.

Round-trip once: encode a known file, decode it, compare size and a quick hash. That proves your copy-paste did not clip characters. Clipped base64 is a classic source of "why is my icon broken" bugs.

Line breaks in some email clients break long base64 strings. Prefer single-line output when a system is picky, or use a file download instead of chat paste for large payloads.
