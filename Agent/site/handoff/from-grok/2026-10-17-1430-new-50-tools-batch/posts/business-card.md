## Hard cases

Two business card outputs named `download (1)` is how wrong packets ship. Rename immediately.

Odd inputs (partial files, mixed orientations, incomplete rows) confuse operators more than the business card UI. Inspect inputs before blaming the tool.

Locale and formatting issues can shift meaning after business card even when the UI reported success.

Some destinations re-encode business card exports—download the stored copy when a portal allows and re-check.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving sample data in a business card run that later gets sent as real.
- Chaining five tools when business card alone would finish the job.
- Overwriting the only master input while exporting from business card.
- Ignoring size/format/length caps until the final business card click fails.
- Sending both draft and final business card outputs without labeling the authority file.
- Skipping the success line and only eyeballing the business card result.
