The letter is a draft made from your answers. Read it, change it to sound like you, and check every fact before you send it. Hiring managers notice copy-paste letters. Put numbers in each result and borrow words from the job post.

## Steps on Cover Letter Maker

1. Open the job post and highlight required skills and outcomes.
2. Open [Cover Letter Maker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/cover-letter-maker).
3. Enter your name, contact lines, company, and job title—required before preview.
4. List best results one per line with numbers. Use language that matches the post without inventing claims.
5. Preview the letter. The tool counts words and flags when you are over a one-page target (about 250–350 words).
6. Download Word for editing (preferred) or PDF if you only need a static copy.
7. Open Word and add one personal sentence—why this company, why this team—only you could write.
8. Fact-check every number against your resume and performance records.
9. Save as `Company_Role_CoverLetter_YYYY-MM-DD.docx` and send with the resume, not as a recycled PDF from last month.
10. Start a new letter for the next job—do not force one draft into five applications.

## Length that works

About 250 to 350 words, one page. Longer letters bury the wins. Shorter letters that only restate the resume waste the page. Aim for three concrete results and a clear ask.

## Common mistakes

- Sending the letter unchanged from the tool.
- Listing duties instead of results (“responsible for accounts” with no numbers).
- Ignoring the job post vocabulary so the letter feels generic.
- One letter for every application with only the company name swapped.
- Over 400 words of biography before the first result.
- Claiming metrics you cannot defend in an interview.

## Interview-proof results

Luis pastes each result line into a notes doc with the source (dashboard export date). If he cannot name the source, the line does not enter the letter. That habit stops resume/letter drift.

## Matching resume and letter

Every number in the letter should appear on the resume or be explainable in one breath. If the letter says "8 points" and the resume says "improved renewals," reconcile the wording before you apply. Recruiters compare both files.

Luis keeps a one-page "evidence" note with links to dashboards so interview stories match the letter.

## When not to force a letter

If the posting says no cover letter, do not attach one. If the portal has essay prompts, answer those instead of uploading a generic letter that ignores the prompts.
