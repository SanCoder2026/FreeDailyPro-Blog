## Steps on Copyright Page Maker

9. Proof for typos twice—front matter errors live forever in first printings.
10. Archive PDF + text with the edition folder.

## What the number line is

Traditional printing line counting down to the current printing. First printing often shows through 1. Turn off if your printer or imprint style does not use it.

## Common mistakes

- Using another publisher’s rights block unchanged.
- Putting an unverified ISBN on the page.
- Expecting the tool to register copyright or get CIP.
- Mismatched trim vs interior file.
- Skipping attorney review when rights are complex (translations, contributions, trademarks).

## Edition discipline

Hardcover, paperback, and ebook may need distinct imprint notes and ISBNs. Generate separate pages per format rather than one generic block for all.

## Translator and illustrator credits

If contributors have separate rights, do not rely on a single generic “all rights reserved” line without review. Capture contributor names accurately; misspellings on the copyright page are painful.

## Year and edition updates

New edition? Update year and printing line. Leaving “First edition 2019” on a 2026 revision confuses libraries and buyers.

## HTML for ebook

Keep the HTML export for EPUB/front-matter paste so print PDF and ebook stay wording-aligned. Divergent copyright text across formats looks unprofessional.

## Permissions and song lyrics

If the book quotes song lyrics or long excerpts, the copyright page is not enough—you need permissions. Templates do not create permission grants.

## Disclaimers for fiction vs nonfiction

Fiction “any resemblance” lines and nonfiction advice disclaimers serve different purposes. Pick language that matches the book type; do not paste both piles of boiler plate blindly.

## Matching printer proofs

When the printer returns a proof, check the copyright page trim and margins. If the PDF was built at the wrong size, regenerate rather than scaling in the print UI.

## Self-pub checklist tie-in

Copyright page sits with ISBN assignment, LCCN (if any), and printing number. Update the whole set when you roll a second printing—not only the cover.

## Team review

Have one person who did not write the book proof the copyright page. Fresh eyes catch missing years and wrong legal names of LLCs.


## Publisher name accuracy

Use the legal publishing entity name buyers and retailers expect—not a nickname. Mismatches between copyright page, KDP account, and bank entity create payment and tax confusion later.
