## Why estimates are a range

Front matter (half title, title, copyright, contents) can run about 4–12 pages. Chapter starts on right-hand pages leave blanks. Dialogue, headings, tables, and images change density. ## Even page totals

Printed books use both sides of a sheet, so the tool rounds up to an even page count. That matches how printers think about signatures and blanks.

## Common mistakes

- Locking spine width on a first estimate.
- Ignoring front matter and chapter blanks.
- Assuming dialogue-heavy books match dense nonfiction density.
- Pasting a manuscript with tracked-change garbage still counted as words.
- Expecting the estimate to replace a typeset PDF.

## Limits and good to know

- Words and paragraphs counted in the browser; text not uploaded or saved.
- Among FreeDailyPro’s 200+ professional tools, this is the words↔pages planning estimator.
- Character widths are averages—not extracted from a specific .otf file.
- Hyphenation and widow/orphan control change real line breaks.

## Reverse planning

If marketing wants “about 280 pages,” use page-target mode to see required words. That sets drafting goals before the middle sags.

## Nonfiction extras

Tables of contents, callout boxes, and code listings inflate page count beyond prose models. Add a buffer percentage for technical books before promising a page count in marketing.

## Series matching

If book two must look like book one on the shelf, match trim and approximate density rather than reinventing a new size for each title.


## Marketing blurbs vs production truth

“300-page thriller” in ad copy should track a real estimate plus buffer, not a hopeful round number. Readers rarely count pages, but printers and spine designers do.

## Mass-market vs trade density

Smaller trims with denser type pack more words per page. If you switch from 6×9 trade to a compact trim mid-project, re-run the estimator before you promise shipping weights or print quotes.

## Paste hygiene

Strip footnotes marked only for draft, delete comment balloons exported as text, and remove repeated chapter title dumps from compile tools. Inflated word counts produce inflated page estimates and wrong paper orders.

## Collaboration note for editors

Share the estimator settings (trim, typeface family, size, indent style) in the style sheet so developmental edits that add 8,000 words update the page plan with the same model.

## Print quote timing

Ask printers for quotes only after the estimator and a rough layout agree within about ten percent. Early quotes on fantasy page counts waste everyone's time.

## Audiobook side plan

Page estimates do not equal runtime, but denser pages often correlate with longer chapters. Use the estimate to sanity-check chapter breaks before narration booking.

**Even pages?** Yes—rounded for two-sided sheets.
