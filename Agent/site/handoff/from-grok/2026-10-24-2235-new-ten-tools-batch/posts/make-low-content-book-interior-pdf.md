## Page types and margins

Page types include lined, dot grid, graph squares, checklist (box + line per row), and blank. The inside margin is at least your margin setting and rises with page count toward usual print-on-demand minimums—on the left of right-hand pages and the right of left-hand pages (mirror). ## Common mistakes

- Odd page counts that leave a useless last side.
- Ignoring binding margin growth on thick books.
- Designing cover spine before interior page count is final.
- Uploading a browser print-to-PDF of a random template with wrong trim.
- Skipping a proof copy.

## Product line discipline

Name files `Trim_Style_Pages_v1.pdf`. When you change line spacing, bump the version so SKUs do not mix.

## Bleed and cover separate

Interiors usually need no full-bleed art for simple line journals; covers do. Do not confuse interior trim with full-wrap cover dimensions.

## Paper colour note

Cream vs white paper changes feel more than line style. Note paper choice in your listing so buyers are not surprised.


## Listing photos vs interior truth

Photo mockups that show ornate headers will disappoint buyers if the PDF is plain lines. Match listing imagery to the actual interior style you generated.

## Series SKU grid

Offer lined and dot grid in the same trim as a pair. Buyers who like your 6×9 lined journal often want a matching dot grid for planning—same spine math, different page type.

## Upload checklist for POD

Confirm page count even, trim matches cover, margins clear automated checks, and fonts/lines print sharp in the proof. Fix issues before paid ads point at the listing.

## Local print shop path

The same interior PDF can go to a local shop for short runs. Tell them trim and page count explicitly; do not assume they will guess from file name.

## Line weight on proof

Some POD presses print light grey lines faintly. If the proof looks ghostly, switch to a slightly darker line setting if the tool allows, or accept that cream paper softens contrast.

## Copyright page still needed

Low-content books still need front matter on many stores. Generate interiors here; build copyright separately so the first sheet is not a surprise blank.

## Thumbnail test

Shrink a page screenshot to phone size. If lines vanish, buyers on mobile store pages will think the interior is blank. Adjust line darkness or spacing before you invest in ads.

## Seasonal variants

A holiday cover on the same interior PDF is a normal tactic. Keep the interior file stable and swap only covers so you do not re-upload twenty interiors that are actually identical.
