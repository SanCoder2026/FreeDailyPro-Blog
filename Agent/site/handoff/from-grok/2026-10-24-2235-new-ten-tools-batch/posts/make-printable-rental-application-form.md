## Common mistakes

- Different questions for different applicants.
- Treating the consent line as a completed screening product.
- Storing completed forms in unlocked email forever.
- Skipping local legal review for fees and protected classes.
- Using one state’s form language in another market without review.

## Limits and good to know

## Consistency wins disputes

When two applicants later compare notes, identical forms show a fair process. Keep a PDF master dated for each property.

## Open-house table kit

Print ten blanks, a clipboard, and pens. Do not photograph completed forms into group chats. Collect sealed or folder them immediately.

## Fee transparency

If you charge an application fee, state amount and refund rules on the form and listing. Surprises here create complaints even when the form is clean.


## Screening partner handoff

If a third-party screener needs forms, send the blank PDF they require or your template—not a chat photo of a half-filled page. Keep chain-of-custody for completed applications like other sensitive files.

## Multi-unit portfolios

Generate one form per property address so the header always matches the unit. Reusing a form with the wrong street address creates void paperwork.

## Digital fill vs print

Word downloads help applicants type; PDF helps print at open houses. Offer both when your audience splits between remote and in-person applicants.

## Retention schedule

Write how long you keep rejected applications and follow it. Indefinite email archives of IDs and income data is a risk pattern this tool cannot fix—but the blank-form approach at least keeps creation off third-party servers.

## Co-applicant packets

When two adults apply together, decide whether each completes a full form or a co-applicant addendum. State the rule on the listing so you do not invent process mid-showing.

## Language access

If you regularly rent to bilingual applicants, keep a reviewed translation—do not auto-translate legal consent lines casually.

## Pet and vehicle sections

Enable only the sections you will actually evaluate. Empty sections applicants must still navigate create abandonment; missing sections you care about create incomplete files. Match the form to your real screening criteria.

## Manager contact block

State how applicants return the form (email, portal, in person) on the first page. Forms without a return path sit in inboxes unfinished.

Date the master template in the footer so staff know which revision they handed out during a busy showing weekend.
