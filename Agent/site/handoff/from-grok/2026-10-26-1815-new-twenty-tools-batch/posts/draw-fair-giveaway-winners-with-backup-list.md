## Fairness you can show on a projector

Paste one name per line or load a guest list. Choose how many winners and backups, then press Draw. Each person can win only once; drawn names are removed before the next pick. Extra tickets: write Ana x3 or Ana, 3 for three chances still limited to one win. The draw uses secure randomness (the same class of source used for strong passwords) and avoids simple modulo bias. Every draw is numbered; discarded draws stay in the record with timestamp, fingerprint of the entry list and method, so re-rolling without evidence is visible. ## Common mistakes

- Re-drawing until a favourite name appears, which destroys trust.
- Forgetting backups when winners leave the venue early.
- Mixing staff names into the public entry list by accident.
- Publishing results without keeping the CSV fingerprint record for sponsor review.
- Allowing extra tickets without explaining the rule on the entry form.

## Publish the rules before you collect names

Fairness starts before the draw. State how many winners, whether staff can enter, how backups are used, when winners must claim, and whether extra tickets exist. Put those rules on the entry form and on a slide you show once before Draw. After the event, store the CSV with the rules PDF in the same folder so a sponsor audit does not require reconstructing chat history.

If your giveaway spans online and on-site entries, merge lists carefully and remove duplicates under a written rule (one win per person is already enforced by the picker once the list is clean).

## Online plus on-site hybrid draws

Merge social entries and door entries into one list with a source column in your spreadsheet first, then paste names only into the picker if rules treat every ticket equal. If online tickets and door tickets have different odds, run two draws and announce both methods. Never hide a weighted rule.

## Recording video of the draw

Many brands record the screen as they press Draw. Keep the CSV anyway—the fingerprint fields outlive a lost video file. Store both in the campaign folder with the prize fulfillment log.
