## Common mistakes

- Assuming maximum historical returns forever
- Ignoring fees and plan rules that differ by state
- Treating calculator output as a guaranteed balance
- Forgetting multiple children share finite monthly cashflow
- Not checking official IRS and plan documents for tax treatment in your year

## Honest limits

General education information only — not tax, legal, or investment advice. Rules and limits change by year and jurisdiction. Verify with official plan documents and a qualified professional when decisions are large.


## Tax year discipline

When citing contribution limits or tax treatment, name the tax year and the official source. Do not invent thresholds.

## Sibling prioritization

Model one child at a time, then reconcile the household budget so the plan is fundable.

## What if you over-save relative to grants

Revisit near college years with real award letters. Plans have distribution rules — read them.
