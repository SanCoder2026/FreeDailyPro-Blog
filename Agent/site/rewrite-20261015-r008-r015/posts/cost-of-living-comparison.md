## Common mistakes

- Comparing a studio lifestyle in one city to a house in another without saying so
- Using outdated national averages instead of current local rent asks
- Forgetting parking, insurance, or transit passes
- Ignoring temporary double rent
- Treating tax differences casually without checking official sources for your situation
- Hiding partner or childcare costs until after you accept

## Honest limits

General information only — not financial, tax, or legal advice. Figures change; re-check near decision day. The tool structures thinking; it does not guarantee happiness or job outcomes.


## Negotiation without fake statistics

Bring rent quotes and commute costs, not invented indexes. Employers respond better to transparent assumptions than to viral city rankings.

## Family and roommate weights

Run lean and comfortable versions if two adults must agree. Pets, schools, and elder care change the dominant categories.

## Remote work location clauses

Some employers limit where you may live. Confirm policy before you optimize cost of living toward a city they disallow.

## After you arrive

Track actual spend for 90 days and compare to the plan. Update emergency funds if reality runs hotter than the sheet.
