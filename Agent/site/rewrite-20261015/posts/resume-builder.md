## Gather content before you open the tool

List roles, dates, and measurable results first. Use honest titles. Prefer education and skills that match the jobs you actually want. Keep a plain notes file with numbers you can defend in an interview.

Write achievements as verb + scope + result when you can: "Reduced invoice cycle from 14 days to 6 for a 40-client book." Vague claims such as "team player" waste space on a one-page layout.

A builder formats what you give it. It does not invent employers, degrees, or metrics. If a bullet cannot survive a reference check, rewrite it or delete it.

## Tailoring without chaos

Keep one master version with full bullets. For each application, adjust the summary and the top two or three bullets to match the posting language—without fabricating skills.

Label exports clearly: Name_Company_Role_Resume.pdf. Do not maintain twenty unlabeled files that you cannot tell apart a week later.

When two roles share 80% of the same content, still change the first screen of bullets. Recruiters skim the top third first.

## Common mistakes and what to check

- Inventing metrics you cannot explain in an interview.
- Packing every skill keyword until the page is unreadable.
- Exporting without opening the PDF (clipped headers, bad page breaks).
- Relying only on the builder when a portal forces its own form fields—still keep a strong PDF for email and referrals.
- Skipping a human re-read after automated grammar suggestions.
- Using an unprofessional email address on the contact line.
- Leaving "Curriculum Vitae" and "Resume" both in the header for no reason.

Print or zoom to 100% once. Check that dates line up and that the contact line is complete. Confirm phone country code if you apply internationally.

## FAQ for first-time exporters

**Do I need a photo on the resume?** Only if the local market expects it. Many US tech roles prefer no photo. Follow the norm for your country and industry.

**What if the builder layout feels too plain?** Plain often reads better than decorative templates that break in ATS text extraction. Clarity beats ornament.

**Can I reuse the same PDF for every portal?** Reuse the master carefully, but retitle the file and adjust the top bullets per role when the job descriptions differ.

**Should I password-protect the PDF?** Usually no for applications—portals need to open the file. Use password tools only when a recruiter explicitly asks for a protected packet.

These answers keep the focus on shipping a readable page, not decorating it.

## Checklist before you hit send

- Contact line complete and professional.
- Dates consistent (no overlapping impossible jobs).
- One clear role target in the summary.
- PDF opens on phone and desktop.
- Filename includes your name.
- No placeholder text left in any section.

Run this checklist every time. It catches more errors than another template swap.
