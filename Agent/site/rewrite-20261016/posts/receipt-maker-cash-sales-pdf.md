[FreeDailyPro.com](https://www.freedailypro.com) -- Cash sales still need paperwork when a buyer wants proof or you need books that make sense. A full POS is overkill for a weekend market. A clear PDF receipt is not.

## Quick answer

Use [Receipt Maker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/receipt-maker) for line items, totals, and seller identity as the live page supports. Log spend with [Expense Tracker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/expense-tracker). Bundle month-end PDFs with [Merge PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf) and [Compress PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/compress-pdf). Among 200+ professional tools, this path fits light sellers.

## Fields that prevent arguments

Date, seller name, buyer name if known, items, quantities, unit prices, tax if applicable, payment method (cash), and a unique receipt number. Skip any and disputes get fuzzy.

## Numbered workflow

1. Collect sale details immediately after payment.
2. Open [Receipt Maker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/receipt-maker).
3. Fill fields; export PDF.
4. Name file with date and receipt number.
5. Optionally log related costs in [Expense Tracker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/expense-tracker).
6. Monthly: merge with [Merge PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf), compress if needed.

Category: [business tools](https://www.freedailypro.com/category/business-tools).

## Common mistakes

- No receipt number sequence.
- Forgetting tax line when required.
- Emailing without saving your copy.
- Reusing the same PDF for different buyers.

## Mini case

A craft fair seller takes cash for three items. Receipt Maker produces a PDF emailed on the spot. Month-end merge creates a bookkeeping packet.
