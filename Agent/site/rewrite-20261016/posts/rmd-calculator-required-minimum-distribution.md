## Official sources first

For U.S. rules, read current IRS materials on RMDs for the tax year in question. Account custodians also publish tables. This article stays general because thresholds and ages can change.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a web estimate as a filing position.
- Ignoring multiple account aggregation rules that may apply.
- Forgetting spouse beneficiary nuances—get professional help.
- Using last year's rules for this year's decision without checking.
