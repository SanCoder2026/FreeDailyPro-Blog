## Slug rules that age well

Lowercase, hyphens, few stop words, no dates unless required. Avoid stuffing every keyword. Prefer meaning over clever spellings that people cannot type.

## Numbered workflow

1. Finalize the working title.
2. Open [Slug Generator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/slug-generator).
3. Generate and trim if overly long.
4. Use [Case Converter](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/case-converter) if you need consistent casing elsewhere.
5. Confirm the CMS accepts the slug.

## Titles versus permalinks

You may retitle for click clarity later. Changing slugs after publish needs redirects. Decide early whether the slug is locked.

## Common mistakes

- Underscores instead of hyphens when the platform prefers hyphens.
- Unicode punctuation left in.
- Updating slug weekly for "SEO experiments."
- Duplicating an existing path.

## Mini case

An editor titles a post with a long colon-heavy headline. Slug generator yields a short hyphen path. CMS locks permalink. Social uses the witty title; URL stays boring and stable.

## FAQ

**Stop words?** Removing "a/the" is fine when readability remains.

**IDs in slug?** Only if your CMS requires uniqueness.
