## What an NDA is for

Define confidential information, limits on use, duration, and return or destruction of materials. It is not a master services agreement, not an IP assignment, and not a non-compete unless those clauses are intentionally included and lawful in your jurisdiction.

## Numbered workflow

1. Decide whether mutual or one-way confidentiality fits the deal.

## When to insist before kickoff

Share marketing rates freely. Share unreleased roadmaps only after signatures. If a client refuses any confidentiality paper on a sensitive build, price the risk or decline. Your portfolio can wait for safer work.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a generator output as jurisdiction-perfect law.
- Signing without reading residual knowledge or residual IP clauses.
- Using an NDA as a substitute for a clear statement of work.
- Forgetting to match legal entity names with invoice names.
- Storing signed PDFs only in email with no backup.

## FAQ

**Mutual vs unilateral?** If both sides share secrets, mutual often fits. If only one side discloses, unilateral may fit—confirm with counsel.

**Do big companies accept freelancer templates?** Sometimes they require their paper. Read theirs carefully; negotiate if needed.

**Electronic signatures?** Follow the client’s accepted methods and keep audit copies.

## Negotiation tips

Ask for clearer definitions of confidential information. Avoid overbroad non-competes hidden inside NDAs if inappropriate. Align term length with project reality. Export and store final signed PDFs with dates in the filename.

## Security after signing

Still use least-privilege file sharing. NDAs do not replace access controls. Revoke shared drives when projects end as the agreement requires.

## Honest product limits

## Cross-border work

Governing law and venue clauses matter when client and freelancer sit in different countries. Templates may not fit every pair of jurisdictions. Escalate to counsel early for cross-border sensitive IP.

## Startups versus enterprises

Startups may accept mutual NDAs quickly. Enterprises often require vendor onboarding portals and their own paper. Budget time for both paths. Do not stall creative work without clarity on which path applies.

## Recordkeeping

Store signed NDAs with project codes. When a project ends, follow destruction or return clauses for confidential files. Calendar a reminder if the agreement requires certified deletion notes.
