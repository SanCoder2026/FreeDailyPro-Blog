## Classic failures

Unquoted commas. Embedded line breaks. Locale decimal commas. Leading zeros on ZIP and account IDs stripped by Excel. Inconsistent column counts.

## Workflow

Export or convert to CSV, open in a plain text view, spot-check quotes and headers, then import. If two versions disagree, [Diff Checker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/diff-checker) on exported text can show silent column shifts.

## Limits

CSV cannot store multiple sheets, rich formatting, or formulas reliably. If you need those, stay in Excel. Use CSV for interchange, not as an archival format for complex workbooks.
