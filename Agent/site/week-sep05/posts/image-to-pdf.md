*Combine phone photos of pages into a single document you can file or send.*

[FreeDailyPro.com](https://www.freedailypro.com) -- Paper still wins in offices and schools. Capture is usually a phone. The deliverable people want is one PDF, not twenty JPEGs. [Image to PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/image-to-pdf) converts one or more images into a single PDF. It is a real top-traffic pattern on FreeDailyPro.

## Shoot for conversion

Even light, parallel phone, enough resolution. Crooked snaps become crooked pages. For document photos, run [Document Scanner](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/document-scanner) first for crop and straighten, then Image to PDF.

## Multi-page discipline

Name files 01, 02, 03. Keep orientation consistent. Convert images first, then [Merge PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf) if you also have existing PDFs.

## Privacy

IDs and medical forms should not go to random upload converters. FreeDailyPro's browser-first model is a practical fit for private paper.

## Limits

Not automatic OCR into selectable text by itself. Blurry photos stay blurry. Photograph, clean, assemble, merge, compress if needed — that chain covers most paper jobs.
