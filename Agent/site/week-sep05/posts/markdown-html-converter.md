*Draft fast in Markdown, convert to HTML when the CMS wants markup.*

[FreeDailyPro.com](https://www.freedailypro.com) -- Markdown is a friendly drafting format. Many CMSs still want HTML. FreeDailyPro's [Markdown ↔ HTML Converter](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/markdown-html-converter) converts between Markdown and HTML instantly so you can draft in one and publish in the other.

## Workflow

Write headings, lists, and links in Markdown. Convert to HTML. Paste into the CMS. If the CMS rewrites markup, convert a sample back and [Diff Checker](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/diff-checker) against your source to see what changed.

## Limits

Complex components, shortcodes, and custom classes may not round-trip. Keep Markdown for content structure; keep platform features in the platform.

## Who benefits

Bloggers, doc writers, developers maintaining README-driven sites. Speed of drafting plus clean conversion beats fighting a WYSIWYG for basic structure.
