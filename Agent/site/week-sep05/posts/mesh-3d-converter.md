[FreeDailyPro.com](https://www.freedailypro.com) -- 3D toolchains disagree on formats. Slicers love STL. Some editors prefer OBJ. Point-rich scans show up as PLY. FreeDailyPro's [3D Mesh Converter](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/mesh-3d-converter) converts between STL, OBJ, and PLY, plus export paths that include GLB depending on the tool's capabilities.

## Why conversion fails in real life

Unit mismatches (mm vs inches). Non-manifold geometry. Huge triangle counts. Texture workflows that STL cannot carry. Conversion does not fix a broken mesh; it changes container and representation.

## Practical maker path

Export from modeler, convert if the slicer demands another format, inspect scale, slice, print a small test. For documentation screenshots of models you may still use image tools; mesh conversion is about the 3D asset itself.

## Limits

Not a full CAD kernel. Not magical repair for every bad scan. Use it to bridge formats when the model is already printable-ish.
