[FreeDailyPro.com](https://www.freedailypro.com) -- Giveaways die when the audience thinks the draw was fixed. You need a clear range, a clear rule, and a number nobody typed while smiling. FreeDailyPro's [Random Number Generator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/random-number-generator) generates numbers in any range, with or without duplicates.

## Fair process

Publish rules first: eligibility, ticket mapping, winner count, duplicate policy, draw time. Draw live or record the screen. Transparency beats vibes.

## Duplicates

Without duplicates for multiple unique winners. Know your mode before celebrating.

## Limits

Fine for community giveaways. Not a certified gaming device for regulated gambling. For access codes, prefer the [Password Generator](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/password-generator).

## Steps

Map entries to a continuous range, publish it, generate winners, match to names, keep a timestamped screenshot. That ends most good-faith disputes.
