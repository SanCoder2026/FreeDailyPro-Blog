## Why on-device matters

Product photos, headshots, and ID-adjacent images are sensitive. Upload-based removers can be fine, but local processing is a clearer privacy story when it works. Verify behavior in your browser tools panel like you would any permission-sensitive feature.

## After the cutout

Convert formats with [Convert Image](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/convert-image). Place cutouts into documents via [Image to PDF](https://www.freedailypro.com/tool/image-to-pdf) when building one-pagers.

## Limits

Hair and semi-transparent edges still challenge models. Busy backgrounds fail more often. Manual touch-up may remain necessary for premium catalog work.
