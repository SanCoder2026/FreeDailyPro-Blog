## What Favorites does

Click the star on any tool. Open Favorites. See your custom set. Reorder or remove anytime. Return tomorrow and the list waits. No more scrolling categories for Compress Image or Merge PDF.

## Why a personal page changes work

Most people have a small always-needed set: compress PDF, resize images, count words, merge documents, generate QR codes. Hunting wastes time. Favorites puts them on one page so every session starts instantly.

## Set up in under a minute

Open FreeDailyPro.com → star 5–15 tools you use most → open Favorites → done. Works with language support so your page appears in your preferred language. Privacy stays the same — file tools still process client-side.
