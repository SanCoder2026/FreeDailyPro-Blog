### Best Practices for Print-Ready Output

To ensure your printed event materials look just as stunning on paper as they do on your digital monitor, keep these professional rules in mind:
1. **Color Modes:** Design in CMYK whenever possible for physical printing to ensure accurate color reproduction.
2. **Resolution:** Always maintain a minimum of 300 DPI for all raster graphics and images.
3. **Bleed Margins:** Add at least a 0.125-inch bleed around your canvas to prevent white edges during cutting.
