## The Critical Role of Documentation in Recruitment

When extending an offer, precision matters. HR teams handle numerous PDFs, certificates, and tax forms daily. Utilizing tools like [Merge PDF](https://freedailypro.com/tool/merge-pdf) allows you to combine offer letters, background check authorizations, and benefits summaries into a single, cohesive document for the candidate.

Furthermore, managing large scanned files or employee handbook PDFs can slow down email delivery. Compressing files using [Compress PDF](https://freedailypro.com/tool/compress-pdf) ensures smooth transmission without hitting email size limits.

## Streamlining Onboarding and Compliance

Onboarding involves gathering signed agreements, tax withholdings, and identification documents. HR managers frequently need to convert various file formats to ensure universal compatibility. 

* **Document Conversion:** Quickly transform employee records using [Word to PDF](https://freedailypro.com/tool/word-to-pdf) or [JPG to PDF](https://freedailypro.com/tool/jpg-to-pdf) to maintain document integrity across all operating systems.
