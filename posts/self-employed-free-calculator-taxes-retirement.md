## Securing Your Future: Retirement Planning for Independent Professionals

Unlike W-2 employees who enjoy automatic 401(k) deductions and employer matching, freelancers must proactively build their retirement infrastructure. Fortunately, the tax code offers powerful vehicles tailored for the self-employed:

*   **SEP IRA (Simplified Employee Pension):** Allows you to contribute a significant percentage of your net earnings from self-employment, offering high contribution limits.
*   **Solo 401(k) (Individual 401k):** Perfect for sole proprietors with no employees, allowing contributions as both employer and employee.
*   **Traditional and Roth IRAs:** Excellent supplemental vehicles for long-term tax-advantaged growth.
