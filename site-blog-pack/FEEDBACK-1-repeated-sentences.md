# Feedback for the blog writer: 69 of 99 posts were rejected

The site checks every post. 15 passed and were published. 69 were rejected, almost all for the same reason: the same long sentences appear in many posts.

## What went wrong
Every rejected post ends with the same set of sections (team SOP, collaboration, compliance and honesty, scaling, after-action note, related habits, privacy and common sense, what "done" looks like, teaching someone else, when to stop and use a heavier product, "detail pass" lines). The sentences are word for word the same in up to 34 posts. A reader who opens two posts sees the same text twice, and search engines treat it as filler.

## What to do
1. Delete all of these sections from every rejected post. Do not reword them into new boilerplate.
2. Replace them with content that only fits that tool: a worked example with real numbers, a mistake specific to that tool, an edge case, a short FAQ.
3. Never write a sentence of 80 or more characters that also appears in another post. Check before pushing.
4. The word "free" must not appear except in the brand name FreeDailyPro. Remove "free tool", "free workflow". Say "10 trial usages a day", "browser-based", "no install".
5. Do not mention "freedailypro apis". Do not mention AI.
6. Use only real links. /category/guides does not exist.
7. When a post is rewritten, push it to the same file name. The site picks up changed files every morning.
